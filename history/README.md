## Where everything went at the switch

- **48 unpushed ideas** from v1.0 (37) and v0.2 (11) moved to drafts/backlog.md, word for word. Only their Serves and Also serves lines changed: pushed items are referred to by ID (for example "Create and manage projects" became S-002), and a stale pointer ("now in draft-v0.1.md") was dropped.
- **31 entries** in those drafts were copies of items already pushed. They were not copied again; they live in plan/.
- **3 overlaps** are flagged in the backlog for a decision (in each entry's Open questions): Project attachments, Project updates log, and Main-project grouping.
- **2 links** waiting for "Live project status timeline" (from Project phases, S-002.1, and At-a-glance project status, S-003.1) became Amendment entries in the backlog.
- The **in-progress draft v0.1.1** became drafts/draft-v0.1.2.md, because v0.1.1 is the switch itself. Its original text is in the commit "Save in-progress v0.1.1 work before the switch".
- The **v0.1 prototype** was saved in versions/v0.1/prototype/.

## Known leftovers

- Links inside v0.1/draft-v0.1.md to objectives.md, problems.md and solutions.md point to their old place at the top level; they are now in plan/.
- The v0.1 entry in versions/log.md names the draft's old path (drafts/v0.1/); it is now history/v0.1/.
- plan/prototype.md says "Access by role and owning general department" is in the current draft; it is now in the backlog.
